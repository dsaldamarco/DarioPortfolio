# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters, design leads and hiring managers deciding, usually in under a minute and often on a phone between other candidates, whether to shortlist Dario Saldamarco for a UX/UI or product designer role. Secondary: peers and collaborators following a link from LinkedIn, Dribbble or the App Store.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Dario Saldamarco, UX/UI designer. Its job is to get him interviews for UX/UI and product designer roles on digital products (not only iPhone/iPad: the site must read as product design end to end). He asked not to lead with his city. Success: a visitor understands within seconds who he is and what he designs, opens at least one case study, and reaches the email or LinkedIn.

## Positioning

A UX/UI designer who takes a digital product from research to finished product and also builds what he designs: trained at the Apple Developer Academy (Naples), he has shipped native iOS apps in Swift/SwiftUI/SpriteKit, including one live on the App Store, and ran field research and usability testing with real users (Grade 6 students and teachers in Pune, India) for a non-profit client. Design decisions are made knowing how they get built.

## Operating Context

- Recruiters scan, they don't read: role, availability and the strongest project must be visible without scrolling far.
- Case studies are judged on problem, role, process, decisions and outcome.
- The site is bilingual (Italian / English). It opens in the browser's language (Italian for Italian browsers, English otherwise), with a manual switch.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Stack: React 19 + Vite + TypeScript, lucide-react icons, deployed on Vercel. Keep it.
- Routes that must keep working: `/` (home), `/projects/orbys-adventure` (full Orby case study), `/you-can-only/support` and `/you-can-only/privacy` (App Store-linked legal pages for You Can Only).
- The rhythm typing mini-game was removed at the user's request (2026-10-28); it remains in git history.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Dario Saldamarco. Illustrated avatar (`public/favicon.png`, `public/dario-icon.png`) is his own mark.
- Voice: human, direct, never self-congratulatory. Headlines must not sound cute or "cringe" (the user rejected "Progetto app che so anche costruire").

## Product Principles

1. Work first: the strongest real project is reachable in one scroll.
2. Show decisions, not adjectives: every claim is backed by a screen, a finding or a fact.
3. Honest scope: say what Dario did in a team versus alone; no invented outcomes.
4. Make contact effortless from anywhere on the page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard reachable, visible focus, 4.5:1 text contrast, reduced-motion respected, no custom cursor replacing the system one.
